About My Professional Profile

I am Alphonce, Mwita Nyakubare, a Tanzanian Information Technology professional with academic and practical experience in ICT education, computer training, digital content creation, computer maintenance and digital technology.

I hold a Bachelor in Information Technology (BIT) from the National Institute of Transport (NIT). My professional experience includes lecturing, computerised accounting training, digital content creation, social media management, computer repair and maintenance.

I also have practical experience in website development, including the design and development of modern, responsive and user-friendly websites and web-based systems. I use web technologies and programming frameworks to develop digital solutions for individuals, institutions and organisations.
